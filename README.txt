Patterns Charity is a compassionate and purpose-driven block-based WordPress theme designed for charities, non-profits, and fundraising organizations. It offers all the tools you need to build a heartfelt and impactful online presence.

== Description ==

Patterns Charity is a heartfelt and flexible Full Site Editing (FSE) WordPress theme, ideal for charities, non-profits, NGOs, and fundraising campaigns. Built with WordPress FSE, it gives you full control to customize headers, footers, templates, and global styles with ease.

This theme comes with purpose-built patterns and layouts designed to highlight donation drives, volunteer programs, success stories, event details, contact forms, and impact sections. Its fully responsive design ensures your website remains accessible and professional across all devices.

Whether you're raising funds, promoting a cause, or organizing community support, Patterns Charity offers a meaningful and powerful platform to engage your audience and amplify your mission.
